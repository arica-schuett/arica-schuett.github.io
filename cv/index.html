Professional Summary

Arica Schuett is a Moreau Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Political Science at the University of Notre Dame. Her research examines electoral behavior, social movements, and Black politics in the United States, with a focus on protest, shifting partisan coalitions, and electoral change. Her work appears in the Journal of Race, Ethnicity, and Politics, Urban Studies, and PS: Political Science & Politics, and is forthcoming in the Journal of Peace Research.
